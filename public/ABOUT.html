Estudely Packet Explorer: licences and limitations

Your capture is processed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and the page makes no requests other than loading its own files.

Packet decoding engine

Captures are decoded by Wireshark 4.4.5 compiled to WebAssembly by Wiregasm 1.9.1. Both are free software under the GNU General Public License version 2: licence text.

Corresponding source code: Wiregasm v1.9.1 (with build scripts and patches) and Wireshark 4.4.5 source.

Known limitations

  • HTTP/2 and HTTP/3 are not shown as request rows. Encrypted traffic (TLS, QUIC, DoH, DoT) is not decrypted.
  • TLS certificates are shown only when sent in cleartext (TLS 1.2 and earlier). Trust is not checked.
  • Names come only from the capture itself (DNS, mDNS, NBNS, DHCP, TLS SNI, HTTP Host). Ports are traffic observations, not open-port checks. No device or OS identification.
  • Files over 1 GiB are refused; large captures take about a minute per 400,000 packets.
